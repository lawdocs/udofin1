## 1. Principles

**Plain English.** Udofin holds as little about borrowers as it can. What belongs to a lender is kept in that lender's compartment and nobody else's. Everything is stored in India.

1. **Udofin keeps minimal borrower data.** Research reports that an LSP must not store personal data beyond the basic minimum (name, address, contact details) needed for its operations, and no biometric data unless a law allows it [confirmed (secondary)]. Today Udofin stores even less:
   - `identity.borrowers` holds an internal id, the link to the sign-in identity, account status and preferred language. **No name, PAN, Aadhaar, date of birth, address or documents.**
   - The borrower's phone number stays in Supabase Auth (`auth.users`).
   - If a journey later needs the borrower's name or address (Planned, Phase 5), those are the outer limit for Udofin's own records.
2. **Lender-owned data is lender-scoped.** Every tenant-owned row carries `lender_org_id`; Row Level Security is forced on data tables; composite foreign keys `(id, lender_org_id)` make it impossible for a row to point at another lender's data (Built, tested: lender A cannot see or change lender B's data).
3. **Lender-owned sensitive data will be encrypted per lender.** KYC artefacts, bureau reports and loan documents will be encrypted at application level with envelope encryption, using a separate data key per lender held in AWS KMS (Mumbai), with HMAC "blind indexes" where a lookup is needed. Planned (Phase 6 for KYC and bureau data, Phase 7 for documents). pgsodium is not used (Supabase marks it pending deprecation [confirmed]). Destroying a lender's key makes its data unreadable ("crypto-shredding"), which supports exit and erasure [recommendation].
4. **KYC, bureau and Account Aggregator data are the lender's records.** They are obtained under the lender's credentials and licences. Udofin orchestrates the screens and, where the lender instructs, stores results only in lender-scoped encrypted storage as the lender's processor. Udofin does no bureau "soft pull" and no Aadhaar OTP in its own name.
5. **India only.** All production data, backups, logs, queues and files stay in India (section 4).
6. **Udofin is not the loan system of record.** Loan balances, schedules and receipts are copies of what the lender's LMS reported; the LMS is the truth.

**Never stored by Udofin:**

- Aadhaar numbers (the lender keeps a masked number or vault reference where its rules require). The API logger redacts `aadhaar`, `aadhaarNumber` and `vid` as a safety net.
- Biometric data or templates.
- Contacts, SMS, call logs, media or files from the device (the app requests none of these permissions and strips them from the Android manifest).
- Card data.
- Raw payment-provider payloads (only a SHA-256 hash and normalised fields, in `payments.provider_events`).
- Bureau reports, KYC documents or bank statements in Udofin's core schemas.

## 2. Classification scheme

The four levels are defined in code (`DataClassification` in `packages/contracts/src/primitives.ts`) and travel with every event in the `dataclassification` attribute of the event envelope.

| Class            | Meaning                                                                                                                         | Examples                                                                                                                                                                              | Handling                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **public**       | May be published                                                                                                                | Published consent notice text; product categories; lender legal names and registration numbers; grievance officer details that law requires to be published; matching-method versions | No restriction on display; changes are versioned                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **internal**     | Business data with no personal data; not for publication                                                                        | Permission catalogue, roles, audit anchors (hash digests), lifecycle reference tables                                                                                                 | Staff only; may appear in logs                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **confidential** | Personal data that is not restricted, or a lender's commercially sensitive data                                                 | Borrower id and status, loan request, application, offer, consent record, audit event, loan read-model, payment intent, lender product drafts                                         | Access by permission and RLS; never in push payloads or URLs; ids only in logs; encrypted at rest by the platform                                                                                                               |
| **restricted**   | Identity documents and numbers, bureau data, bank account details, contact details, and anything that must never appear in logs | Phone and email, KYC artefacts, PAN, bureau report, bank account verification, call recordings, secrets                                                                               | All of the above, plus: field-level encryption with the lender's key for lender-owned data (Planned, Phase 6); every read logged; never in logs, events or analytics; redacted by the API logger (`services/api/src/logger.ts`) |

Railway sealed variables for staging only                                                                                                                                                                                        | Planned (Phase 3/4)                                            |
| AUTH | Supabase Auth in the same Mumbai project                                                                    | Borrower phone numbers; staff email and MFA factors                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Planned (Phase 3)                                              |

Further rules:

- **Railway (staging) holds synthetic data only.** It has no India region, and it retains logs and traces in a location it does not document [unverified; assume outside India].
- **Queues** run inside Postgres (pg-boss and an outbox table), so they inherit the database location. No Redis or hosted workflow services for personal data.
- **Push notifications** pass through Expo, FCM and APNs outside India: payloads carry no personal data, only a generic message and a deep-link id.
- **SMS** goes through DLT-registered Indian providers; message content is kept minimal. Provider data location is to be checked per provider (Phase 3).
- **AI inference**, if used, runs India-hosted (for example Amazon Bedrock India geo cross-Region inference [confirmed in research]) or receives prompts stripped of personal data (Phase 9).
- **Supabase Edge Functions**, if used, are pinned to ap-south-1 (by default they run nearest the caller [confirmed]).
- **Disaster recovery** copies go to AWS ap-south-2 (Hyderabad) later. Data processed outside India must be deleted there and brought back within 24 hours under the RBI rule as reported [confirmed (secondary) in `rbi-digital-lending.md`; likely in `infra-stack.md`].
- **Production data is never copied to local or staging.**

## 9. Deletion and erasure

- Borrower rows are referenced with `on delete restrict`, so a borrower cannot simply be deleted while lender records point at them. Erasure will be a workflow: close the account, delete or de-identify what only Udofin holds, keep what law requires (with legal holds), and send deletion requests to each lender and processor. Research recommends a deletion-propagation API with a lender webhook within 24 hours and a deletion certificate [recommendation]. Planned (Phase 5).
- Consent withdrawal is recorded as a new `withdrawn` record (append-only, Built). Stopping further sharing with that lender when consent is withdrawn is Planned (Phase 5).
- Lender exit: export the lender's data, then crypto-shred its keys [recommendation]. Planned (Phase 6 keys; exit runbook Phase 11).
- Append-only tables (`consent.records`, `audit.events`, `audit.anchors`, `lending.application_events`, `servicing.schedule_*`, `payments.provider_events`) refuse every update and delete, for every role. Retention purges on these tables therefore need a controlled, audited procedure that keeps the audit anchors verifiable. Open design item (Phase 2).
