# RBI (NBFC – Credit Facilities) Directions, 2025: Chapter III, Digital Lending

**Instrument:** Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Credit Facilities) Directions, 2025, RBI/DOR/2025-26/347, DOR.CRE.REC.No.266/07-01-008/2025-26, 28 November 2025, **updated as on 1 April 2026**.
**Source file:** [`../sources/rbi-nbfc-credit-facilities-directions-2025-updated-2026-04-01.pdf`](../sources/rbi-nbfc-credit-facilities-directions-2025-updated-2026-04-01.pdf), pages 14–28.
**Status of this note:** read directly from the primary text by the engineering agent; paragraph numbers are as printed. This is the first set of regulatory facts in this repository that rests on a primary source. It is **not legal advice**: where a design consequence needs a legal view it is flagged for counsel. Pages 4–13 (definitions) and 29–54 are covered in [`05-credit-risk-and-credit-facilities-other-chapters.md`](05-credit-risk-and-credit-facilities-other-chapters.md).

## 2. What your decisions mean for the design

Your decisions on 13 October 2026: Udofin provides a **5% DLG** and acts as the lenders' **recovery agent**; the legal entity is **Infrabyx Consultancy OPC Private Limited**.

### 2.1 Guarantee (DLG) ledger: a new core module

The text turns DLG from a commercial promise into a **ledger with hard rules**, which the system must enforce rather than leave to spreadsheets:

- A DLG is **per lender and per DLG set**; the set is a frozen list of sanctioned loans (para 24(2)).
- **Cap = 5% × cumulative amount disbursed from the set.** It rises as loans are disbursed, does **not** fall when loans repay (Case 1), is reduced by invocations, and **is not restored by recoveries** (para 25(4)).
- **Available cover = cap − cumulative invoked.** The ledger must refuse an invocation above available cover.
- **Invocation deadline:** the lender must invoke within **120 overdue days** (27(1)); the system raises alerts at configurable thresholds (for example day 90 and 110) and records each invocation with evidence.
- **Form of cover:** cash with the lender, FD with lien, or bank guarantee only (23). The ledger tracks each instrument (amount, bank, lien or guarantee reference, expiry) and **checks that cover instruments last at least as long as the longest loan tenor** in the set (27(2)). **Udofin never holds the guarantee cash itself.**
- **Borrower liability is untouched by invocation** (25(2)): invoking DLG must **not** change the borrower's balance, schedule or reported status; the servicing read-model must keep the two apart.
- **Recovery sharing** (25(3)) is contractual: the ledger stores the agreed formula and computes amounts due between lender and Udofin after each recovery.
- **Public monthly disclosure** (28): number of portfolios and amount of each, **within seven working days of month-end**. The system generates the figures; a person approves and publishes them. A missed deadline is an alert, not a silent failure.
- **Auditor-certified declaration** (20(3)): aggregate DLG outstanding, number of lenders and portfolios, past default rates. The system can produce the numbers; the statutory auditor certifies them.

### 2.2 Recovery agent: design consequences of the text

The text expressly contemplates an LSP acting as recovery agent (6(5)) and lays down these constraints. Udofin's own design follows them by default:

1. **Prior notice to the borrower.** Particulars of the recovery agent must reach the borrower by **email/SMS before first contact**, and again when the agent changes (9(6)). The collections module therefore has a state "agent notified" that **blocks every outbound collections contact until the notice is sent and its delivery is recorded.**
2. **Money goes only to the lender.** Repayments are made **directly into the NBFC's account with no pass-through or pool account of the LSP** (10(2)); the flow of funds must not be controlled by the LSP (10(3)). Collections are therefore **payment links and mandates in the lender's name**; Udofin collects _contact and follow-up work_, not money.
3. **No collection fees from borrowers.** Udofin's fees for recovery (and for everything else) are paid **by the lender** (10(4), 10(5)). The system has **no field for charging a borrower a collection fee** and rejects any ledger line that would route a Udofin fee to a borrower.
4. **Cash recovery is a separate, off-by-default capability.** Para 10(5) lets the **NBFC** use a physical interface to recover **cash** from delinquent borrowers and requires the receipt to be **reflected in full in the borrower's account the same day**. Whether Udofin's own field staff count as the NBFC's "physical interface", and who may hold cash for how long, is **a question for counsel and each lender**. Until answered, cash collection stays disabled in the product.
5. **Conduct standards** (calling hours, language, harassment, penal-charge disclosure, agent certification, call recording) sit in the **Responsible Business Conduct Directions, 2025** and later amendments. Those documents were **not supplied**. Until they are, the collections module is specified only to the extent of the paragraphs above (see section 7).
6. **Conflict of interest.** Udofin will be **both loss-absorber (DLG) and collector**. The text does not forbid this, but the lender stays "fully responsible and liable for all acts and omissions of the LSP" (6(7)) and must monitor conduct (6(3)–(4)); lenders will therefore want audit access, complaint monitoring and **limits on recovery incentives**. The architecture separates the DLG ledger from the collections work queue, records every contact, and does not let collections staff see or influence DLG invocation decisions.

### 2.3 The legal entity (an OPC)

Para 19 requires the DLG-providing LSP to be "incorporated as a company under the Companies Act, 2013". An One Person Company is a company under that Act (confirm with counsel). The points the text makes **practical** for an OPC, to be put to your counsel and compliance owner, not assumed:

- **Capacity to honour the guarantee** (20(3)): cover must sit in cash, a lien-marked FD or a bank guarantee **from day one of disbursement**, rising with disbursement. For a lender portfolio of ₹100 crore, 5% is ₹5 crore. The entity's capital and bank-guarantee limits set how much portfolio it can support; the DLG ledger will show headroom against **committed cover**.
- **Auditor certification** of the declaration needs a **statutory auditor** (20(3)).
- **Whether a one-member company may be the contracting party** for lenders' due diligence (6(2): "ability to comply with all applicable regulations") and whether OPC-specific restrictions affect carrying on guarantee or recovery business: counsel.

### 2.4 Data, consent and permissions: confirmed, no change

Paragraphs 13–14 and 16 confirm what the baseline already implements or plans: no contacts, files and media, call logs or telephony; one-time camera, microphone or location only for onboarding or KYC with explicit consent (the mobile build blocks the others); consent with an audit trail, purpose shown at each stage, revocable, with deletion on request; minimal LSP storage limited to "name, address, contact details, etc."; no biometrics; **servers in India, with offshore processing deleted and brought back within 24 hours**.

One refinement: **para 14(1) lists name, address and contact details as the basic minimum an LSP may keep.** The baseline's borrower table stores none of these (the phone number sits only in Supabase Auth). Phase 05 will add name and address as lender-scoped, lender-readable fields, not as an Udofin-owned profile.

### 2.5 Offer comparison: confirmed

Para 7 confirms the comparison-screen rules in ADR 0022 to the word, including that **ranking by a publicly pre-disclosed metric is permitted** (7(4)). The documented, consistent matching mechanism (7(2)) must be versioned in the database and shown in the app's disclosure page.

### 2.6 Grievance: confirmed, with one precision

Para 12 requires a **nodal grievance officer at both the NBFC and the LSP that interfaces with the borrower**, displayed on websites, the DLA and **inside the KFS**, with complaint lodging in the app and responsibility remaining with the NBFC; after rejection, dissatisfaction or **30 days' silence** the borrower may go to RBI's CMS. The Equator design (ADR 0018) is unchanged: it can never sit before or instead of this route.
