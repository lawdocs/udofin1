# Extracts from primary regulatory sources

Each file here was produced by reading the corresponding PDF in [`../sources/`](../sources/README.md)
and records, paragraph by paragraph, what the text says, who bears it, and what it means for Udofin
as an LSP, a default-loss-guarantee (DLG) provider and a recovery agent.

These extracts are working notes for engineering and compliance. They are **not legal advice**, they
quote or paraphrase closely but are not a substitute for the instrument, and they must be reviewed
by the compliance owner and counsel before a contract, policy or launch decision relies on them.
