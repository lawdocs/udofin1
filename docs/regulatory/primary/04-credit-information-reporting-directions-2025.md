## 1. Applicability and definitions

**Commencement.** Para 2: "These Directions shall come into force with immediate effect, unless otherwise specified." No paragraph in the main body specifies a later date for any obligation. There is no "1 July 2026" or weekly-reporting provision in this text (searched the full text for "July", "2026", "incremental", "amendment": only hit is "July 01, 2018" in para 12(8)).

**Who is a "Credit Institution" (para 3(1)).** "These Directions shall be applicable to following Non-Banking Financial Companies [hereinafter collectively referred to as 'Credit Institutions (CIs)' and individually referred to as 'Credit Institution (CI)'], for all layers unless specified otherwise": (i) NBFC-D; (ii) NBFC-ICC; (iii) NBFC-Factor; (iv) NBFC-MFI; (v) NBFC-IFC; (vi) IDF-NBFC; (vii) HFC; (viii) NBFC-P2P; (ix) Core Investment Company. (Each "registered with the RBI" under the stated Act.)

**Not applicable (para 3(2)).** (i) MGC; (ii) NBFC-AA; (iii) SPD; (iv) NOFHC; "(v) NBFCs not having any customer interface." A Note says applicability follows the Registration, Exemptions and Framework for Scale Based Regulation Directions, 2025.

**Definitions (para 4).**

- 4(1) "Company": Companies Act 1956 s.3 or the corresponding section of the 2013 Act.
- 4(2) "Credit Information Companies (CICs)": companies granted a certificate of registration under s.5 CICRA. The table lists four: CRIF High Mark Credit Information Services Pvt Ltd (25 Nov 2010); Equifax Credit Information Services Pvt Ltd (26 Mar 2010); Experian Credit Information Company of India Pvt Ltd (17 Feb 2010); TransUnion CIBIL Ltd (5 Mar 2012).
- 4(3) "Specified User (SU)": "shall have the same meaning assigned to it under clause (l) of section 2 of CICRA and those notified under regulation 3 of the Credit Information Companies Regulations, 2006, hereinafter referred to as 'CIC Regulations' (as amended from time to time)."
- Para 5: words not defined here but defined in CICRA, the CIC Rules 2006 and the CIC Regulations carry those meanings; other words take their meaning from the RBI Act 1934, Banking Regulation Act 1949 or Companies Act 2013.

**Digital lending / LSP.** The text defines nothing about digital lending apps (DLAs), lending service providers (LSPs), loan service providers or digital lending. The words "digital", "lending service provider", "LSP" and "DLA" do not appear in the main body or Annexes (text search). The only outsourcing reference is para 13(4)(i) (SHG data, quoted below). Para 22 lists other NBFC Directions to be complied with for credit information reporting, including the Credit Facilities Directions, 2025 (22(6)) and the Credit Cards Directions (22(1)); this text does not say what they require.

**Obligation bearer.** Every obligation in this text is addressed to a CI (the NBFC) or a CIC. No obligation is addressed to an LSP, a recovery agent or any service provider. Para 5 (and the Note under para 3) do not extend it to them.

## 4. What this means for Udofin's bureau access design (interpretation, anchored in quoted text)

**Can an LSP itself pull a credit report?** This text does not say. It contains no rule on who may obtain a credit information report (CIR) beyond: (a) para 4(3) defines "Specified User" by reference to CICRA s.2(l) and CIC Regulations reg 3, and (b) para 20 requires a CI to provide in its loan policy "for obtaining CIRs from one or more CICs". The access rules for specified users sit in CICRA and the CIC regulations/Directions, which are outside this PDF. Consequence: this instrument neither authorises nor prohibits an LSP pull; it is silent. The prior position in our docs (Udofin pulls only with each lender's member credentials, report lands in the lender's system) is not contradicted by this text, but this text does not confirm it either. Counsel to confirm against the CIC Directions 2025 and CICRA.

**What the text requires when a report is obtained.** Para F is a single paragraph, para 20, quoted above. It does not state: any consent requirement for a bureau pull; any customer notice at the time of the pull; any "purpose" limitation; who within the CI or its agents may access; retention or deletion periods for obtained reports; record-keeping of enquiries; any role for an LSP or service provider; or any bar on "soft" enquiries. Para 11's "consent clause" language concerns a CI sharing credit information with CICs (reporting), not obtaining a report. The customer alerts in para 34(1)(i) are triggered when the CI submits default/DPD information to CICs, not when a report is pulled.

**Design implications (analyst view).**

- Keep bureau pulls on the lender's member credentials until counsel confirms otherwise (CRI-01 stands, still not confirmed by this text).
- Do not rely on para 11 as authority to skip borrower consent or notice for a pull.
- Para 38(5) (best practice, board-approved): first-time borrowers with no credit history should not be rejected for that reason alone. Any Udofin offer-ranking or pre-screen must not treat "no bureau record" as a decline.
- Para 38(4): full identifier data to CICs (PAN, Voter ID etc.). Udofin-captured identifiers need to be complete and consistent, subject to the data-minimisation rule (the lender, not Udofin, stores the KYC artefacts).

## 7. Open questions for counsel

1. **Weekly reporting and amendment.** Does the 1st Amendment (reported ~4 Dec 2025) exist in final form, and does it apply to NBFCs from 1 Jul 2026 with weekly incremental files and the 9th/16th/23rd/last-day dates? This text, effective immediately, has fortnightly reporting with a 7-calendar-day window. Which text governs after 1 Jul 2026?
2. **CKYC field.** Is a standalone CKYC-number field mandatory anywhere after 28 Nov 2025, or only the "CKYC" ID-type catalogue value reported "if available"?
3. **LSP pulls.** Under CICRA and the CIC Directions 2025, may an LSP initiate a bureau enquiry as the lender's agent (using the lender's member credentials) without being a specified user? What agreement and access controls are required? This text is silent.
4. **Pre-pull notice and consent.** Where is the requirement to notify the borrower and obtain consent before a CIR pull, and what record must be kept? The CRI-04 wording is not supported by this text.
5. **Para 11.** Confirm it dispenses with consent only for the CI's reporting to CICs and has no bearing on consent for obtaining reports or for collections contact.
6. **Agent liability.** The text puts all duties on the CI. Para 13(4)(i) states outsourcing responsibility only for SHG data. Confirm that outsourcing/recovery-agent Directions make the lender answerable for Udofin's reporting inputs and grievance delays, and how to allocate compensation costs by contract (para 35).
7. **Settled/disputed status.** Does the "disputed" reporting for wrong debits (para 12(4) Explanation) apply to collections disputes (for example, charges contested during recovery)? What customer notice is required before reporting "settled"?
8. **Compensation data.** Para 35(8) requires a complaint form to capture bank/UPI details. Can Udofin's grievance module capture them consistently with DPDP and our minimisation rule, or must the lender's form do it?
9. **Notice of default alert.** Para 34(1)(i): who sends the alert (lender or Udofin as agent), in what language, and does a collection communication before the alert breach any fair-practices expectation?
10. **Cross-references.** Para 22(6) (Credit Facilities Directions, 2025) and para 22 generally: which paragraphs carry DLA credit-reporting duties (CRI-05) and must be added to this primary set?
11. **Internal Ombudsman / RB-IOS coverage.** Which of our partner lenders are covered under RB-IOS 2021 and the Internal Ombudsman framework (paras 36-37), and does Udofin's grievance workflow need to show those stages?
12. **Unread items.** Annex I Form 3 field-level pages (printed pp. 36-61) and Annex III (pp. 63-68) were skimmed from a text extraction, not read as images; not reviewed for LSP relevance beyond keyword search.
