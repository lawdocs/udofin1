# Primary-source extract: RBI NBFC Know Your Customer Directions, 2025 (updated 29 December 2025)

- **Instrument:** Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Know Your Customer) Directions, 2025 (Updated as on December 29, 2025)
- **Reference:** RBI/DOR/2025-26/361, DOR.AML.REC.No.280/14.01.003/2025-26, 28 November 2025 (signed Veena Srivastava, CGM). One later insertion is marked in the text: the Explanation to para 63(9), "inserted with effect from December 29, 2025 vide ... (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Know Your Customer) Amendment Directions, 2025 dated December 29, 2025".
- **Source file:** `docs/regulatory/sources/rbi-nbfc-kyc-directions-2025-updated-2025-12-29.pdf` (96 pages: Chapters I-XI pp 3-69; Annex I (MHA UAPA Order, 2 Feb 2021) pp 70-83; Annex II (MoF WMD Order, 1 Sep 2023) pp 84-96).
- **Method:** Read in full via the PDF text layer (all 96 pages, including both Annexes). The page image of p 29 (para 25, OTP e-KYC limits) was inspected to confirm the figures. Other page images were not separately inspected. The document has almost no tables, so no table was at risk of garbling. Paragraph numbers are as printed. **Unreadable:** only the Hindi title line on p 1 (Devanagari glyphs did not extract); nothing in the operative text was unreadable.
- **Status label for everything quoted here:** confirmed (primary text). Statements about what Udofin "must do" or "may do" are our reading (interpretation) and need counsel review (section 9).
- **Read in full but only summarised:** paras 30-38 (sole proprietors, companies, partnerships, trusts, beneficial owners; relevant only if Udofin ever onboards MSME or entity borrowers), paras 64, 69, 71 (FATCA/CRS, wire transfers, third-party products; no Udofin role), Annexes I and II (sanctions freezing procedures).

Important framing: these Directions bind the **NBFC (the lender)**, not Udofin. The words "Lending Service Provider", "LSP", "agent" (in the sense of an onboarding agent), "business correspondent", "DLA" and "digital lending" **never appear**. The only outsourcing wording is para 13 (decision-making cannot be outsourced) and para 27(1)(i) (V-CIP technology outsourcing and no data retention by the vendor). Udofin's permitted role must therefore be inferred from those two provisions, from para 22 (reliance), and from the lender's outsourcing and digital-lending rules (see `02-outsourcing-directions-2025.md` and `01-credit-facilities-chapter-III-digital-lending.md`). Where we say a paragraph "reaches" Udofin, that is an inference.

## 1. Applicability

- Para 3: applies to "all categories of Non-Banking Financial Company ... for all layers, unless specified otherwise". Proviso: "not applicable for 'NBFCs not having any customer interface'." Note to para 3 ties applicability to the Registration, Exemptions and Scale Based Regulation Directions, 2025 (not read). **Housing finance companies are not mentioned here**; they are not excluded from this text, but whether a separate HFC KYC instrument applies is not answered in this document (open question Q8).
- Para 4: applies to overseas branches and majority-owned subsidiaries, to the extent not contrary to host-country law; the stricter standard wins.
- Para 5(2)(xii): "Regulated Entities (REs)" is defined across all RBI-regulated categories (banks, AIFIs, NBFCs, ARCs, PSPs/PPI issuers, authorised persons). The Directions themselves speak of "the NBFC" throughout.
- Para 5(1)(xvi) "Person" and 5(2)(ii) "Customer": "a person who is engaged in a financial transaction or activity with an NBFC and includes a person on whose behalf the person ... is acting."
- Para 75: the Directions are "in addition to, and not in derogation of" other laws (PMLA, Aadhaar Act, IT Act).
- Para 73-74: replaces earlier KYC directions for NBFCs (circular DOR.RRC.REC.302/33-01-010/2025-26 dated 28 Nov 2025); past actions stay governed by the repealed text.

## 4. Customer identification: what must be collected and how (Chapters V, VI)

### 4.1 When identification is needed (para 21)

Para 21: commencement of an account-based relationship (1); international money transfer for a non-account holder (2); doubt about "authenticity or adequacy of the customer identification data" (3); selling third-party or own products above ₹50,000 (4); walk-in transactions of ₹50,000 or more (5); suspected structuring below ₹50,000 (6). For a loan, (1) applies.

### 4.2 Documents for individuals (para 23)

The NBFC "shall obtain" for an individual (or a beneficial owner, authorised signatory or PoA holder of a legal entity):

- (1) the **Aadhaar number** where the person wants a benefit or subsidy under a scheme notified under s.7 of the Aadhaar Act, or "decide[s] to submit their Aadhaar number voluntarily to a NBFC or any RE notified under the first proviso to sub-section (1) of section 11A of the PML Act"; or
- (2) the proof of possession of Aadhaar where the NBFC can carry out **offline verification**; or
- (3) proof of possession of Aadhaar where offline verification cannot be done, or any **OVD** or equivalent e-document with identity and address; or
- (4) the **KYC Identifier** "with an explicit consent to download records from CKYCR"; **and**
- (5) the **PAN** or equivalent e-document, or **Form No. 60**; and
- (6) "such other documents including in respect of the nature of business and financial status".

Provisos (as printed):

- (i) if the customer submits an Aadhaar number to an NBFC notified under the first proviso to s.11A(1), "such NBFC shall carry out authentication of the customer's Aadhaar number using UIDAI's e-KYC authentication". A customer may give a different current address by self-declaration.
- (ii) offline verification must be carried out where proof of possession is submitted and offline verification is possible.
- (iii) for an equivalent e-document of an OVD, verify the digital signature under the IT Act and take a live photo per para 24.
- (iv) for an OVD or Aadhaar proof where offline verification cannot be carried out, verify through Digital KYC (para 24).
- (v) for a KYC Identifier, retrieve records online from CKYCR "in accordance with paragraph 62" (**printed cross-reference; CKYCR is para 63, para 62 is FCRA; see Note A below**).
- A transitional proviso allows, "for a period not beyond such date as the Government may notify for a class of REs", a certified copy of the Aadhaar proof or OVD plus a recent photograph instead of Digital KYC. No such date appears in the text.
- Exception handling for those who cannot do e-KYC "owing to injury, illness or infirmity" (benefit/subsidy cases): offline verification preferably, or certified copy of another OVD, "An official of the NBFC shall invariably carry out CDD done in this manner", with a "centralised exception database" (grounds, customer details, authorising official) subject to concurrent audit and supervisory review.

Explanations to para 23:

- **Explanation 1:** where the customer submits proof of possession of Aadhaar containing the Aadhaar number, "ensure that such customer redacts or blacks out his Aadhaar number through appropriate means where the authentication of Aadhaar number is not required as per proviso (i) above."
- **Explanation 2:** "An NBFC official can perform biometric-based e-KYC authentication, including Aadhaar Face Authentication."
- **Explanation 3:** use of Aadhaar and proof of possession "is in accordance with the Aadhaar ... Act, 2016 and the regulations made thereunder."
- **Explanation 4:** "Aadhaar number is not mandatory for purposes of KYC. However, in case the customer is desirous of receiving any benefit or subsidy under any scheme notified under section 7 ... the customer shall provide the Aadhaar number to the NBFC. In other cases, customers may provide the Aadhaar number voluntarily."

Definitions that matter (para 5(1)): "Offline verification" and "Aadhaar number" take their Aadhaar Act meanings (xv, i); "OVD" (xiv) lists passport, driving licence, "proof of possession of Aadhaar number", Voter ID, NREGA job card, NPR letter, and Aadhaar proof "in such form that the UIDAI issues" (xiv)(a); "deemed OVDs" for address only (utility bill not more than two months old, property or municipal tax receipt, pension orders, employer allotment letters) with a current-address OVD due within three months (xiv)(b)-(c); "equivalent e-document" (x) includes DigiLocker-issued documents; "Digital KYC" (viii) is captured by "an authorised officer of the NBFC" with live photo and latitude/longitude.

### 4.4 Digital KYC (para 24), summarised

Authenticated NBFC application at customer touch points; access controlled for authorised officials; live photo watermarked with CAF number, GPS coordinates, official's name, employee code, date and time (24(4)); only live photos captured, white background, no other person in frame (24(5)); live photo of original OVD captured from above, no skew (24(6)); QR auto-population allowed (24(8)); OTP to the customer's own mobile as signature on the CAF, **not** the official's mobile (24(9)); official's declaration verified by OTP and live photo (24(10)); transaction-id given to customer (24(11)); official verifies match between document, live photo and CAF (24(12)); CAF digitally signed by the official, printed, signed by the customer, scanned and uploaded (24(13)). This is an assisted process; we do not build it unless a lender asks and supplies the authorised-official model.

Reading: the cap is **term loans only, ₹60,000 aggregate sanctioned in a year** (the text says "in a year", not "a financial year"; the unit of the year is not defined). It limits the amount a lender may sanction to the same customer on this KYC route, across loans. A revolving line or credit line is not a "term loan". Udofin's offer engine must carry a per-lender flag "KYC route = OTP e-KYC" and the lender must enforce the ceiling (we show it, the lender enforces it). The conditions in para 25 do **not** apply when OTP e-KYC is used for updation or periodic updation (para 42(3)(v)).

### 4.6 Non-face-to-face onboarding by other routes: enhanced due diligence (para 44)

Applies to non-face-to-face modes "other than customer onboarding in terms of paragraph 25", including "CKYCR, DigiLocker, equivalent e-document, etc." and certified copies for NRIs/PIOs. Required: (1) if V-CIP exists, offer it "as the first option to the customer for remote onboarding"; (2) "alternate mobile numbers shall not be linked post CDD ... for transaction OTP, transaction updates"; transactions "only from the mobile number used for account opening"; Board-approved policy on change of mobile number; (3) current-address proof **and** "verify the current address through positive confirmation before allowing operations in the account" (address letter, contact point verification, deliverables); (4) PAN obtained and verified from the issuing authority's facility; (5) "First transaction in such accounts shall be a credit from existing KYC-complied bank account of the customer"; (6) categorise as **high-risk** and "enhanced monitoring until the identity of the customer is verified in face-to-face manner or through V-CIP".

Drafting note: items (5) and (6) read as written for deposit-style accounts. How a lender applies "first transaction ... a credit" to a loan disbursal into the borrower's own KYC-compliant bank account (our probable reading) is for the lender and counsel (Q4). Udofin must not assume item (6) is waived for DigiLocker or CKYCR journeys.

## 5. Video-based CIP (V-CIP), paras 26-27

**What V-CIP may do (para 26):** new-customer CDD for individuals, proprietors, authorised signatories and beneficial owners (1); proprietorships also need e-documents of activity proof (2); conversion of OTP e-KYC accounts (3); updation and periodic updation for eligible customers (4). Para 5(2)(xiv) treats compliant V-CIP "on par with face-to-face CIP".

**Infrastructure, para 27(1):**

- (i) NBFC must comply with RBI's baseline cyber security framework; "shall house the technology infrastructure on its own premises and the V-CIP connection and interaction shall necessarily originate from its own secured network domain"; technology outsourcing must follow RBI guidelines; cloud: data ownership "rests with the NBFC only", all data including video transferred to the NBFC's "exclusively owned / leased server(s) including cloud server ... immediately after the V-CIP process is completed" and the CSP or third-party technology provider "shall retain no data".
- (ii) end-to-end encryption between customer device and hosting point; consent recorded "in an auditable and alteration-proof manner".
- (iii) capable of "preventing connection from IP addresses outside India or from spoofed IP addresses".
- (iv) recordings carry live GPS co-ordinates, date and time stamp; video quality adequate to identify "beyond doubt".
- (v) face liveness / spoof detection and face matching, though "the ultimate responsibility of any customer identification rests with the NBFC". Facial gestures are not mandatory; special needs must be considered.
- (vi) AI may be used "to ensure that the V-CIP is robust".
- (vii) update on forged-identity attempts and near-misses; report detected forged identity "as a cyber event".
- (viii)-(ix) VA, penetration test and security audit by **CERT-In empanelled auditors**, gaps closed before roll-out, repeated periodically; functional, performance and maintenance testing of the application and APIs.

**Procedure, para 27(2):** clear SOP; "operated only by officials of the NBFC specially trained" (i); one video file, fresh session on call drop (ii); vary question sequence and type (iii); reject on prompting (iv); factor in existing, rejected or negative-list customers (v); official records audio and video, captures a photograph, and obtains identification using one of (a) OTP based Aadhaar e-KYC, (b) offline verification of Aadhaar, (c) CKYCR records using the KYC identifier (printed as "paragraph 62", see Note A), (d) equivalent e-document of OVDs including DigiLocker (vi); redact Aadhaar number (vii); offline XML or Secure QR "not older than three working days from the date of carrying out V-CIP" (viii); video within three working days of downloading from CKYCR, Aadhaar authentication or e-document (ix); capture current address if different and confirm economic and financial profile (x); capture a clear image of PAN or accept e-PAN, "verify the PAN details from the database of the issuing authority, including through DigiLocker" (xi); "use of printed copy of equivalent e-document, including an e-PAN is not valid for the V-CIP" (xii); official ensures photo match and detail match (xiii); accounts opened through V-CIP "operational only after ... concurrent audit" (xiv); comply with IT Act (xv).

**Records, para 27(3):** "store the entire data and recordings of V-CIP in a system / system located in India" with date and time stamp (i); preserve the activity log "along with the credentials of the official performing the V-CIP" (ii); the general record-management rules (section 8) also apply.

Consequence for Udofin (our reading): V-CIP is an official-operated, lender-hosted process. A shared Udofin-hosted video service is difficult to square with "house the technology infrastructure on its own premises", "its own secured network domain" and "retain no data". If a lender wants V-CIP inside our app, the call must terminate on lender infrastructure and our app can only be the shell (Q5).

## 13. Obligations that matter to Udofin, ranked

1. **Never decide KYC compliance** (para 13; para 18 officer records rejection reasons): approve, reject or mark "KYC complete" only by a lender actor.
2. **No Aadhaar authentication in Udofin's name** (para 23 proviso (i)); only a s.11A-notified NBFC authenticates, under its own credentials. Redact or black out any Aadhaar number received (Explanation 1; para 27(2)(vii)); keep no full Aadhaar number (our rule 3).
3. **OTP e-KYC loans are capped:** term loans only, ₹60,000 aggregate per year, full CDD within one year or no further debits, specific consent, declaration of no other OTP-based account, OTP flag on CKYCR upload (para 25(1), (5)-(8)).
4. **V-CIP data belongs to the lender; Udofin retains none** (para 27(1)(i)); V-CIP infrastructure sits on the lender's own premises or exclusive cloud and network domain; recordings stored in India (27(3)). Udofin keeps only reference and outcome.
5. **Non-face-to-face onboarding by CKYCR, DigiLocker or e-documents is enhanced due diligence** (para 44): offer V-CIP first if available, no alternate mobile after CDD, current-address positive confirmation, PAN verified at source, high-risk until face-to-face or V-CIP.
6. **Alerts and OTPs only to the Aadhaar-registered or account-opening mobile; no mobile-number changes without the lender's Board-approved process** (paras 25(2), 44(2)). Udofin's phone-OTP login and notifications must follow the lender's number, not a later number.
7. **CKYCR:** the lender uploads within 10 days, communicates the KYC Identifier, updates within seven days of new information and retrieves updates; Udofin must support the lender seeking the KYC Identifier first and not re-collecting documents (para 63(1), (6), (7), (9)).
8. **PAN verified against the issuing authority's facility** at onboarding, V-CIP, EDD and updation (paras 17(10), 27(2)(xi), 44(4), 42(5)(ii)); the lender chooses the channel. Loan-account credits stay allowed if operations are ceased for no PAN (para 43).
9. **Records: five years after the relationship ends (identity) and from the transaction (transactions)**, held by the lender, retrievable swiftly (para 47); Udofin supports export and retention without becoming the record-keeper. Honour the lender's legal hold during any deletion job.
10. **Periodic updation and secrecy:** support lender-run updation (self-declaration through app permitted, 42(3)); log three advance notices and three reminders including letters (42(7)); never disclose risk category or suspicion (paras 19, 20(4), 52); never use KYC data for cross-selling or group referral without the customer's express permission (para 61(2)).
