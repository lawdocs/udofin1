# Udofin regulatory register

**As of:** 10 October 2026 · **Register version:** 1.0 (first issue) · **State:** draft for counsel review · **Companion:** `docs/regulatory/controls-matrix.md`

## 1. Read this first: research limitation and verification gate

- **No primary regulatory text was opened.** During research the sandbox could not reach rbi.org.in, rbidocs.rbi.org.in, uidai.gov.in, cert-in.org.in, npci.org.in or the other primary sites (WebFetch DNS failure; the proxy refused HTTPS), and the shared web-search budget ran out. The only pages read directly were technical vendor pages (Apple and Android developer sites, Supabase, Railway, AWS and Expo documentation, npm packages).
- **What the entries rest on.** Search-result snippets and secondary sources: law firms, compliance vendors, lender-published notices and the business press.
- **Verification gate (release gate).** Every instrument, date, reference number and paragraph number below must be checked against the primary RBI, UIDAI, MeitY, NPCI, CERT-In or store text before anyone relies on it in a contract, a policy, a disclosure or a launch decision. No entry may be relabelled "confirmed against primary text" until counsel or the compliance owner has read that text and recorded the check in the change log (section 5).
- **This register does not state that Udofin is compliant.** It records which instruments matter, what they govern for Udofin, how sure the research is, and what to do next. The controls that answer each requirement are in `docs/regulatory/controls-matrix.md`.

### Labels used

| Label          | Meaning (carried over from the research notes)                                                                                                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| confirmed      | Several independent sources agree, or a snippet came from the issuer's own page. It is **not** a reading of the primary text unless the entry says so. |
| likely         | Secondary source(s) or the researcher's recollection; not re-verified in the session.                                                                  |
| interpretation | The researcher's reading of how a rule applies; no source states it directly.                                                                          |
| unverified     | Could not be checked. Treat as an open question.                                                                                                       |
| draft          | The instrument itself is a draft or proposal, not law. Shown in the "Effective" column.                                                                |

Where two research notes disagree, both labels are shown. "Reference no." is filled in only where a research note states it; otherwise it reads "to verify".

**Applies to:** RE = the regulated lender carries the legal duty (it reaches Udofin through the lender's contract); LSP = Udofin in its role as lending service provider and operator of the digital lending app (DLA); both = RE and LSP; platform = binds Udofin directly as a company or technology operator, whatever the lenders do; app store = store policy binding Udofin as app publisher.

## 4. Change management

### 4.1 Where changes come from

The compliance owner (see 4.3) watches, at least monthly and whenever an alert arrives:

- RBI notifications, Master Direction pages (including new "updated as on" versions of A01–A04), press releases, drafts and FAQs;
- UIDAI, MeitY (DPDP, Aadhaar), CERT-In, NPCI, TRAI, CERSAI, SEBI (for pledges) and NeGD/API Setu notices;
- Google Play and Apple policy pages and policy timelines;
- court judgments relevant to ADR, stamping and consumer protection;
- notices and contract changes from partner lenders, and their compliance teams' flow-down requests;
- the compliance-tracking feed from LawDocsComply, where a lender or Udofin uses it.

Each item is logged as a new register entry or a proposed change to an existing one, with the source link and the date seen.

### 4.2 How a change is assessed

1. **Verify the text.** Obtain the primary instrument. Counsel or the compliance owner records the paragraph references and whether each existing label still holds. Drafts are recorded as drafts and do not trigger build work unless the owner decides to prepare early.
2. **Assess the impact** using this checklist. Record the answer to every line, including "no impact".

   | Area                  | Questions                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Where it lives in Udofin                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
   | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
   | Products              | Which product categories and lender products are affected? Does a platform floor or availability flag change? Does any lender need a new product version?                                                                       | `lending.product_categories` (changes only by reviewed migration); `lending.loan_products` and `lending.loan_product_versions` (approved versions are immutable; new versions need maker-checker approval) — `supabase/migrations/20261010000400_lending_core.sql` |
   | Lender configurations | Which lenders are affected, by lender type and governing instrument (A01–A04)? Which capability flags (Aadhaar route, OVSE, RB-IOS coverage, Internal Ombudsman) change? Does each lender's compliance officer need to approve? | Lender master data (Phase 04); `tenancy.organizations`                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
   | Disclosures           | KFS template, offer-card fields, privacy policy, data-storage policy, consent notices, grievance page, store listings, website pages                                                                                            | Consent notices are immutable once published, so a wording change is a **new notice version** (`consent.notices`, `supabase/migrations/20261010000300_identity_consent_audit.sql`)                                                                                 |
   | Workflows             | Application lifecycle, KYC orchestration, grievance clock, collections windows, payment and mandate flows, AI assistant limits                                                                                                  | Application transitions table and its mirror in `packages/domain` (both tested; `packages/domain/test/lifecycle-sync.test.ts`)                                                                                                                                     |
   | Data                  | What is collected, stored, retained or shared; residency; DPIA update                                                                                                                                                           | Data inventory; retention schedule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
   | Contracts             | Lender–LSP agreement, data processing agreement, vendor contracts and flow-down clauses                                                                                                                                         | Contract register                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
   | Tests                 | New or changed pgTAP and unit tests; CI gates; regression of existing tests                                                                                                                                                     | `supabase/tests/`, package tests                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
   | Evidence              | What record will prove the control works, who keeps it and for how long                                                                                                                                                         | Controls matrix "Evidence retained" column                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
   | Controls matrix       | Which rows change; which new rows are needed                                                                                                                                                                                    | `docs/regulatory/controls-matrix.md`                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
   | Timing                | Effective date, transition period, lender deadlines, app-store review lead time                                                                                                                                                 | Register "Effective" column; section 3                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

3. **Classify** the change: no impact; disclosure or content only; configuration; code; contract or policy; or a combination. Set a target date ahead of the effective date and create the phase or backlog item.
4. **Implement and verify** through a reviewed pull request with tests, and capture the evidence named in the matrix.
5. **Communicate** to affected lenders, operations staff and, where needed, borrowers.
6. **Close** by updating this register, the controls matrix and the change log in the same pull request.

### 4.4 How this register is versioned

- The register lives in the repository at `docs/regulatory/register.md` and changes only through reviewed pull requests, each with a compliance reviewer.
- Version numbers are MAJOR.MINOR with a date. MAJOR changes when an instrument is added or removed, or when a label changes after primary verification. MINOR changes cover wording, next actions and links.
- Every label change records who checked the primary text, which document and paragraph, and the date. A label is never upgraded without that record.
- Review cadence: monthly scan; whenever RBI publishes a new version of A01–A04; before every release that touches a regulated flow; a full review each quarter; and the verification gate before launch.
