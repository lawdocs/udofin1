# Phase 07 specification: Key Facts Statement, loan documents, acknowledgement and offer acceptance

**Status:** draft v1 for owner approval (14 Oct 2026). **Nothing in this phase is implemented.** Per the workflow in `CLAUDE.md`, no code is written until you approve this spec and answer (or defer) the questions in section 14.
**Depends on:** Phases 01–06 (all on branch `claude/peaceful-babbage-14p1ci`, draft PR #1).
**Roadmap:** `docs/product/roadmap.md`, Phase 07. Disbursement, repayment and servicing are **out of scope** (Phase 08).
**Regulatory basis read for this spec:** `docs/regulatory/sources/rbi-nbfc-responsible-business-conduct-directions-2025-updated-2026-04-01.pdf` (the Master Direction you uploaded; re-read in full for paragraphs 29–30 and Annex I on 14 Oct 2026), the filed Credit Facilities Directions (digital-lending chapter), and the earlier research notes. Internet research added only secondary sources (section 3.4); the RBI website could not be opened from this environment.

## 1. Objective in plain English

After a borrower has picked a lender's offer (Phase 06), the **lender** gives Udofin the facts of that loan in a structured form. Udofin turns them into the regulator's standard **Key Facts Statement (KFS)** in a PDF, **without changing a single number**, keeps the exact file, shows it to the borrower, explains each section in plain English or Hindi, and records the borrower's explicit "I have read and understood" action. Only then can the borrower **accept** the offer, after a fresh one-time code bound to that offer. After acceptance the lender's approved **loan agreement** (with the KFS repeated as a summary box) is generated from the same facts, reviewed by the borrower, and handed to an **e-signature boundary** that stays inactive until a provider and legal method are approved (D-13). Every document is stored privately, versioned, never overwritten, delivered through approved channels only, and every access is logged.

Udofin does not decide credit, set or recalculate terms, approve its own documents, sign for anyone, or move money.

## 2. Assumptions (to be confirmed in section 14)

1. The lender, not Udofin, is the author and approver of every legal and financial document; Udofin is the workflow and rendering technology (`CLAUDE.md` rule 1).
2. The first covered lenders are NBFCs (MGM Financiers, Megapode Vyapar). The only KFS rule set that is confirmed in primary text on file is the NBFC one. Banks, UCBs and other lenders get **no** rule until their own text is read and approved (section 5).
3. No lender-approved template, no approved Hindi wording, no working-day calendar, no e-sign provider, no email provider and no production key management exist today. The build therefore ships **engines, gates and sample-only test fixtures**, and the production workflow stays blocked (section 12).
4. Test fixtures that look like legal documents are labelled "SAMPLE — NOT A LEGAL DOCUMENT" and exist only in tests and local development.

### 3.2 Annex I — the standard KFS, as it will be modelled

**Part 1 (interest rate and fees/charges).** 1 loan proposal/account number and type of loan · 2 sanctioned amount (₹) · 3 disbursal schedule (stages, or 100% upfront; clause reference if staged) · 4 loan term · 5 instalment details (type; number of EPIs; EPI amount; commencement of repayment after sanction) · 6 interest rate (%) and type (fixed / floating / hybrid) · 7 for floating rate: reference benchmark, benchmark rate B, spread S, final rate R = B + S, reset periodicity (months), impact of a 25 bps change in R on EPI and number of EPIs · 8 fees/charges, split into **payable to the NBFC (A)** and **payable to a third party through the NBFC (B)**, each one-time or recurring with amount or percentage (processing fees, insurance, valuation, any other) · 9 APR · 10 contingent charges (penal charges for delayed payment, other penal charges, foreclosure, switching floating/fixed, any other).

**Part 2 (qualitative).** 1 loan-agreement clause on recovery agents · 2 clause on grievance redressal · 3 phone and email of the nodal grievance officer (a generic email is allowed if a response follows within one working day) · 4 whether the loan is, or may become, subject to transfer or securitisation · 5 collaborative lending details (originating RE, funding proportion, partner RE, blended rate) · 6 digital loans: cooling-off/look-up period with no prepayment penalty, and details of any LSP acting as recovery agent.

**Para 29(3) computation sheet.** Sanctioned amount; loan term; instalment structure; interest rate type and rate; **total interest over the tenor**; fees payable (A, B); **net disbursed amount** (sanctioned amount minus fees); **total amount to be paid** (principal plus interest); **APR**; schedule of disbursement; due-date format DDMMYYYY. The text's own example notes that the sum of rounded instalments (₹23,280) can differ from principal plus interest (₹23,274), explained as rounding: a lender-supplied note, not a Udofin correction.

**Amortisation schedule.** Per instalment: number, outstanding principal, principal, interest, instalment.

### 3.3 Gap analysis against what Phases 01–06 already store

The Phase 06 offer (`lending.offers`) carries principal, tenure in days, instalment frequency/count/amount, annual rate, APR, processing fee, an opaque `other_charges` list, total repayable, a single penal-charges sentence, validity and a lender-hosted `kfs_url`. **It cannot produce an Annex I KFS.** Missing: proposal number, loan type, disbursal schedule, repayment commencement, interest-rate _type_ and the floating-rate table, fees split by payee and timing, itemised contingent charges, total interest, net disbursed amount, the amortisation schedule, every Part 2 item, and the cooling-off period. Udofin must **not** derive them. They must come from the lender (section 6.1).

Other facts that shape the design:

- Step-up (`identity.stepup_challenges`) already has actions `offer_acceptance` and `esign`, a context reference, hash-only codes, five tries, five minutes and single use. No action consumes it yet.
- The application lifecycle already has `offer_accepted → agreement_pending → agreement_executed` with `agreement_executed` allowed only for `system`; one accepted application per request is enforced by a unique index. Offer status already has `accepted`.
- `onboarding.documents` and the `DocumentStore` port give private opaque-key storage with SHA-256, but the only implementation is a **development-only local-disk mock**; no encrypted production store exists (D-06, D-47).
- The consent notice machinery (`consent.notices`, with review status and per-lender wording) can carry versioned acknowledgement wording.
- Nothing in the repository renders PDFs. No working-day calendar exists. No email address is collected or verified; no SMS provider exists (D-35).
- Phase 06 left D-16 (default order) and D-37/D-42 (lender consent wording) **unapproved**. The decision register (`docs/product/open-decisions.md`) holds no approved answer to D-13, D-16, D-25 or D-37, so none is assumed.

Open applicability questions for counsel (D-56): NBFC-P2P (para 4 says only paras 98–100 apply, so no KFS), microfinance loans, gold loans (para 49 mentions the KFS), HFC-specific variants, "retail" and "MSME term loan" boundaries, revolving lines.

Rules are read by the API and database; UI components never hard-code a regulatory rule.

## 6. Data model (all forward-only migrations; every table: RLS enabled and forced, explicit grants, comments, pgTAP incl. cross-tenant negatives)

Schema `documents` (new). Money is integer paise, rates are basis points, times are `timestamptz`, dates shown in IST.

### 6.1 Lender-supplied KFS data package — `documents.kfs_inputs`

One row per **offer revision** and package version; append-only; owned by the lender (`lender_org_id`, composite FK to `lending.offers`). Content is `kfs_data_v1` JSON mirroring section 3.2 (every Annex I item, the computation sheet fields and the amortisation schedule rows), plus `source` (`manual_portal`, `signed_callback`), `submitted_by`, `submitted_at`, `content_sha256`. Validation (structural, never corrective) runs on intake and is stored beside the row: `validation` = `accepted` / `held`, `issues[]` as codes. A held package is never rendered. Fields the lender omits stay `null`; **no default of zero**. Borrower identity text in the package (a name "as per lender records") is allowed only as an optional rendered field and is never logged, evented or audited in clear.

Consistency checks that **flag for lender correction** (never change values): the package's sanctioned amount, tenor, instalment amount/count, APR and total repayable equal the linked offer revision's values; schedule rows are in sequence, dates valid and ascending; the sum of schedule principal equals the sanctioned amount; net disbursed equals sanctioned amount minus the sum of fees the lender lists (a comparison of lender-supplied figures, not a Udofin calculation of fees); third-party charges present in the schedule of fees appear in the APR basis as stated by the lender; every mandatory Part 1/Part 2 field in the rule's `required_fields_version` is present; a floating rate carries the item-7 table. A difference explained by the lender's own rounding note (as in the Annex example) is accepted only if the note is present (tolerance rule: D-61). Udofin does **not** recompute APR, interest, EMI or the schedule, and never shows its own numbers beside the lender's.

### 6.2 Templates — `documents.templates`, `documents.template_versions`

`template_key`, `document_type`, `language`, `lender_org_id` (null only for a regulator-prescribed layout such as Annex I), `version`, `body` (a structured layout definition, not free HTML), `body_sha256`, `status` (`draft` → `lender_approved` → `compliance_approved` → `retired`), approvers, `approved_at`. A template that is not approved for the lender, language and rule cannot be used. **Udofin ships no lender legal text.** It ships the Annex I _layout_ as a `draft` regulator-format template needing compliance approval, and an agreement _engine_ with no clause text. A missing template makes the document type `not_ready` with the approval it needs named.

### 6.3 Documents — `documents.items`, `documents.item_events`

A row is one **immutable version** of one document: `id`, `document_type`, `version`, `lender_org_id`, `application_id`, `borrower_id`, `offer_id` (the revision), `kfs_input_id`, `template_version_id`, `rule_id`, `proposal_no`, `language`, `storage_key` (opaque), `sha256`, `size_bytes`, `generated_at`, `generated_by` (`system`), `approval_status` (`draft`, `lender_review`, `lender_approved`, `rejected`), `approved_by`, `approved_at`, `approved_sha256` (must equal `sha256`), `issuance_status` (`not_issued`, `issued`, `superseded`, `withdrawn`, `expired`), `issued_at`, `supersedes_id`, `superseded_by_id`, `idempotency_ref`, and, for a KFS, `content_digest` (SHA-256 of the canonical KFS data used, stored also on the agreement's summary box). Triggers reject any update other than the status columns, forbid delete, and forbid issuing unless `approval_status = 'lender_approved'` and `approved_sha256 = sha256`. The enforced vocabulary mirrors the brief: Udofin-generated draft → lender-reviewed → lender-approved → issued → borrower-acknowledged → executed → superseded / withdrawn / expired. Acknowledged and executed are facts held in their own tables, so the document row itself is never edited after issue.

### 6.4 Validity — `documents.validity`

Separate from the offer. One row per issued KFS: `kfs_issued_at`, `kfs_valid_from`, `kfs_valid_until`, `kfs_rule_id`, `min_working_days`, `calendar_id`, `basis` (`tenor_ge_7` / `tenor_lt_7`), and the offer's own `offer_issued_at`, `offer_valid_until` (read from the offer, not copied as a KFS field). Acknowledgement, acceptance and execution timestamps live on their own rows. The API and database check **both**: acceptance needs `now() <= kfs_valid_until` **and** `now() <= offer_valid_until`. An offer whose `valid_until` falls before the KFS validity end is flagged for lender correction (the lender is bound for the whole KFS window); Udofin never extends either date.

`documents.working_day_calendars` / `working_days`: calendar id, date, `is_working`, source and approver. **No calendar is seeded.** Without an approved calendar for the lender, KFS generation stops with `calendar_missing`; calendar days are never substituted. Proposed interpretation (D-53, counsel to confirm): the validity runs from the instant the KFS is made available to the borrower to the end (23:59:59 IST) of the Nth working day after the day of issue; the lender may set a longer window, never a shorter one; the minimum is computed from tenor ≥ 7 days or < 7 days using the lender-stated tenor in days. Boundary and holiday cases are tested.

### 6.5 Acknowledgement — `documents.acknowledgements`

Append-only. `borrower_id`, `application_id`, `offer_id`, `document_id`, `document_sha256`, `kfs_rule_id`, `template_version_id`, `language`, `wording_notice_id` (a versioned `consent.notices`-style record; wording must be `approved` or the step is blocked), `wording_sha256`, `explanation_version`, `sections_viewed` (the sections the app actually displayed), `action` (the exact button or checkbox used), `acknowledged_at`, `auth_aal`, `auth_context`, `channel`, `app_version`, `audit_event_ref`, `idempotency_ref`. **Never inferred** from a download, a screen open or navigation; nothing is pre-ticked; one acknowledgement per (document, borrower); a new KFS version needs a new acknowledgement.

### 6.6 Acceptance — `documents.acceptances`

Append-only; one per offer revision (unique). Holds offer id and revision, KFS document id and sha256, acknowledgement id, step-up challenge id, `accepted_at`, `idempotency_ref`, `application_status_after` and the explicit field `signature_status = 'not_signed'`, `disbursement = 'not_started'`. Created only by one database function (section 8). Acceptance never equals execution or disbursement.

### 6.7 Signing — `documents.signing_requests`, `signing_events`

States (mirroring the brief; no vocabulary pre-exists): `not_required`, `awaiting_initiation`, `ready_for_signing`, `initiated`, `awaiting_borrower`, `executed`, `failed`, `expired`, `cancelled`, `requires_reconciliation`. Columns: `provider_key`, `provider_mode` (`mock` only possible in local/test), `provider_ref`, `document_id`, `document_sha256`, `signer_method_ref` (null until D-58), `evidence_ref`, timestamps, `uncertain_since`. A request references an issued agreement version; `executed` can be set only by a verified provider callback or reconciliation lookup (never by OTP, never by the app). On `executed` the system moves the application `agreement_pending → agreement_executed` (already system-only).

### 6.8 Delivery and access — `documents.deliveries`, `documents.access_log`

`deliveries`: `document_id`, `channel` (`in_app`, `sms_notice`, `email_notice`; the last two only through an approved port), `destination_ref` (a reference to the verified destination, never the address in clear), `requested_at`, `provider_accepted_at`, `delivered_at` (only when a provider confirms), `failed_at`, `failure_class`, `attempt`, `idempotency_ref`. Notification text carries no document content, amounts or links with embedded credentials ("Your documents are ready in the Udofin app"). `access_log`: who (borrower or lender user), document, version, `purpose` (`view`, `download`), time; **no content and no phone**. "Borrower accessed" is recorded only when an authenticated retrieval actually streamed bytes.

### 6.9 Reuse and links

`lending.offers` gets one nullable link `kfs_document_id` (already present) set when a KFS is issued for that revision. The Phase 06 comparison keeps its lender-hosted link as the fallback until a generated KFS exists; once an issued KFS exists, the comparison's KFS link opens the in-app authenticated viewer. Offer values are never touched.

## 7. Document services and workflow

### 7.1 Generation (worker, idempotent)

Job `document.generate` keyed by `(offer_id, document_type, language, kfs_input_id, template_version_id)`; a retry returns the existing row. Preconditions, checked in one function that returns a coded refusal (`lender_inactive`, `product_inactive`, `offer_not_current`, `offer_withdrawn`, `kfs_input_missing`, `kfs_input_held`, `applicability_unresolved`, `template_not_ready`, `calendar_missing`, `validity_below_minimum`, `approval_required`): lender approved and active; product version active; offer current, not withdrawn and not expired; KFS data package accepted; one approved applicability rule; approved template for lender and language; calendar present; mandatory fields complete; computed KFS validity at or above the rule floor; offer validity covers the KFS window.

Rendering is deterministic over the stored inputs. The proposal number (Annex I item 1) is allocated once per offer revision at first generation, stored, and reprinted on every re-render; rule: D-51 (lender-supplied, or Udofin-allocated `{lender_code}-{random}`; never reused). The PDF bytes are hashed, written to the private `DocumentStore`, and the row is inserted in the same logical step; a crash between them leaves an orphan object found by a sweep, never a half-issued document. Failure is recorded on a `generation_failures` row with a class and a retry count; **the offer and any earlier document are untouched**.

### 7.2 Approval and issuance

Generation produces `draft`. A lender user with a new permission `document.approve` (aal2, not the person who entered the data package unless the lender has one user; segregation per ADR 0025 offered as a lender setting, D-46) reviews the exact PDF and approves it, which records `approved_by`, time and `approved_sha256`. Issuance (`issued`, validity stamped) is a separate step. **No automatic issuance** unless the lender has signed a delegation recorded by platform staff against contract evidence (`delegation_mode = delegated`, off by default, D-57). Udofin never uses lender logos, seals or signatures unless the lender uploaded them under an approved template. The printed KFS carries the issuing lender's name, the proposal number, the validity window, "Prepared with Udofin as the lender's service provider", and no claim of RBI approval.

### 7.3 Supersession, withdrawal, expiry

A corrected KFS or revised offer creates a **new** document (and, if the offer changed, a new offer revision from Phase 06), links `supersedes_id`, marks the old version `superseded` (still retrievable, still the acknowledged version for its acknowledgement), and requires a **new acknowledgement**. Withdrawing an offer withdraws its issued documents; expiry sweeps (`validity` and `offer.valid_until`) mark documents `expired` and block acceptance. An agreement is never regenerated with newer numbers after review: any change goes through supersession and re-acknowledgement.

### 7.4 Agreement and other documents

Catalogue `documents.document_types` (seeded rows are only the _types_, all `not_ready`): `kfs`, `kfs_summary_box`, `loan_agreement`, `sanction_letter`, `repayment_schedule`, `terms_and_conditions`, `privacy_and_data_use`. Each type declares data source, issuing authority (lender), applicability rule, required approvals, delivery requirement and a retention class **placeholder** (no period; D-25). The loan agreement is generated **after acceptance** from the lender-approved template and the _same_ `kfs_input` snapshot: the summary box is rendered from the stored KFS data and carries the KFS `content_digest`; generation refuses if the digest differs from the acknowledged KFS (`summary_box_mismatch`). The agreement may only be generated for the acknowledged, accepted KFS version.

## 8. Offer acceptance

One database function `lending.accept_offer(offer_id, kfs_document_id, acknowledgement_id, idempotency_ref)` (borrower session, aal2), single transaction, with `identity.stepup_consume('offer_acceptance', offer_id)` inside it. It verifies, under row locks: the borrower owns the application; the offer is the current `issued` revision, not withdrawn, `valid_until` not passed; the KFS document is the current issued, non-expired version for that offer and its validity has not ended; an acknowledgement exists for exactly that document hash and borrower; the template/rule/offer versions agree; the step-up challenge is verified, unexpired, unconsumed, for this action **and this offer revision** (an old verification cannot authorise another offer or revision); no other offer on the request was accepted (the existing unique index makes the second acceptance fail with `another_offer_accepted`). It then sets the offer to `accepted`, moves the application `approved → offer_accepted` (borrower, already allowed), records the acceptance row and an audit event, and enqueues `document.generate` for the agreement (a separate job, so a generation failure cannot undo or block the recorded acceptance; the application is `agreement_pending` once the system starts it). A repeated call with the same idempotency reference returns the first result. The lender sees "offer accepted by borrower; agreement pending". Whether acceptance needs a further lender confirmation before the agreement is a lender setting, off by default (D-62).

## 9. Electronic signature boundary

Port `SigningProvider` (`initiate`, `status`, `cancel`, `fetchEvidence`) in `packages/adapters`, with a mock that is clearly labelled, usable only in `local` and `test`, refused in staging and production by `assertProviderAllowed` and by a database check on `provider_mode`. States as in 6.7. The mock's outcomes (completed, failed, expired, **unknown**) exist so the reconciliation path is tested; an unknown outcome becomes `requires_reconciliation` and is resolved only through the provider's status lookup, never by a blind re-initiation. The step-up action `esign` is required to start signing, but an OTP is **not** a signature and the UI says so. Until D-13 and D-58 are decided the borrower sees "Signing is not available yet" and the application stays `agreement_pending`. Stamping is recorded as `stamping_status = 'not_assessed'`; no stamping integration (D-58).

## 10. Delivery, retrieval, storage, retention and security

- **Retrieval:** authenticated API streams bytes from the private store with `Cache-Control: no-store`, an opaque id in the URL, ownership checked server-side on every request (borrower: own documents; lender: documents of its applications, via `application.read`). No public or permanent object URL; if an object store later supports presigned URLs, only ≤5-minute links created per request. Storage keys are opaque and never contain names, PAN or lender identifiers.
- **Delivery:** the in-app "My documents" is the base channel. SMS and email notices go through ports only; no provider exists, so those paths answer `not_configured` and are never reported as sent. Registered-and-verified destination handling for email is not built (Udofin collects no email today): D-59.
- **Encryption:** documents are stored under per-lender keys through the key-management port. Only the local development adapter exists, so **production document storage is blocked** until D-47 and D-06 are resolved; the API refuses to issue in staging/production without a real encrypted store.
- **Retention:** each document and evidence row carries a `retention_class` and a `legal_hold`; **no periods, no deletion job** until D-25 is decided. A borrower deletion request (Phase 05 processor) records "retained, pending D-25" for these artefacts and anonymises only Udofin-held profile data. Superseded versions are never deleted merely because a newer one exists.
- **Logs and events:** no document content, amounts, names, phone or storage keys in logs, outbox payloads, push or errors (jobs carry ids only).
- **Audit:** every approve, issue, supersede, withdraw, acknowledge, accept, signing event, delivery attempt and access writes an audit row with identifiers only; document-related tables are append-only with triggers; the audit table protections from Phase 02 apply.

## 11. Borrower and lender interfaces

**Borrower (Expo app, English and Hindi; real backend calls):** offer and documents review (one screen per document with the lender's name, version and validity), KFS viewer (the actual PDF, plus the same facts as readable sections), explanation by section (static explanatory text keyed to Annex I fields, with the borrower's own values from the stored package, and a clear label that the explanation does not change the terms), acknowledgement (unticked, explicit button, wording version shown, disabled until the sections are displayed and the document has been opened), acceptance with step-up code, agreement review (summary box on top), signing status (not available / initiated / failed / reconcile), delivery confirmation, **My documents** and document history (versions, superseded, expired, withdrawn), expired/withdrawn offer, failed or pending signing. No countdown timers, no urgency language, no hidden terms; validity is shown as a plain date and time in IST. Hindi: UI strings in Hindi; legally significant content (KFS body, acknowledgement wording, agreement) only in languages with an **approved** template/wording; otherwise the approved language is shown with a plain notice that the Hindi version is awaiting approval.

**Lender portal:** KFS data entry (a structured form or JSON upload validated against the schema, with a clear issue list), document list and detail with the exact PDF, approve/reject/withdraw/supersede, acceptance status, signing and delivery status, template status ("not ready: needs lender approval"). **Platform:** applicability rule proposals/approvals, template approvals, calendar import and approval (API first, as in Phase 06; ops screens only if you ask).
