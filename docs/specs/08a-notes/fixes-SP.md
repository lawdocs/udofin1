## Owner decisions (safest behaviour implemented)

1. Payment-kind refunded, disputed or returned: exception for a person, nothing booked, because the amount on a payment object may be the whole payment or the refund. Providers should send refunds and chargebacks as their own objects.
2. A refund or chargeback that names no payment is booked only when the intent has exactly one provider payment. The adapters must send `parentPaymentRef`.
3. After a captured payment the loan accepts no new payment until the lender reports something newer than the capture (`stale_data`); this can delay a legitimate second payment. Foreclosure is open alone; installment and overdue are not open together.
4. An in-flight attempt holds an intent for 24 hours after its last event (a chosen constant). A capture after expiry is recorded and raises `payment_on_closed_intent`.
5. Money received on a loan the lender closed, or for a foreclosure after its quotation lapsed, marks the intent succeeded and opens an exception (the provider's money is on record). The alternative is to hold the intent pending.
6. Any refund, chargeback or reversal, partial included, makes the borrower stage needs_attention.
7. A receipt the lender contradicts is hidden rather than shown as under review.

## Consequence for a delivery that arrives for an inactive configuration

The receiver verifies signatures only against ACTIVE configurations, so a delivery for a suspended or retired configuration reaches the database as unverified or unmatched and is only a receipt (`token_unknown_or_inactive`); the database cannot tell it from garbage and this is kept. What changed is the exposure: links already issued are withdrawn at once (take, detail and purge), so a borrower is not sent to pay into a destination the platform has just declared invalid. A payment completed before the suspension through a link already open at the provider is not recorded as provider_received; the intent expires and the settlement report (reconciliation) is the backstop. Cancelling or expiring a suspended configuration's open intents belongs to the accounts area.
