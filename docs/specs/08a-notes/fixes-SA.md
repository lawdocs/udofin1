## 4. Decisions for the owner

1. Registry fingerprints: a rejected or revoked LENDER-owned entry releases its fingerprint (A14), but an entry that is NOT lender-owned (udofin, group_company, other) keeps it for good, whatever its status. Reason: those entries exist so that an account is recognised and refused for lenders; letting a revocation free the fingerprint would let the same real account be registered afterwards as a lender's account. A mistaken registration of a non-lender account therefore cannot be corrected by re-registering it; that fails closed.
2. One active configuration per lender is strict (any provider mode). The brief said "per lender and provider mode", but payments and mandates count ALL active configurations of the lender and refuse more than one (`config_ambiguous`), so a mock and a production configuration active together would already break the lender.
3. Holds are lender-wide: while a hold stands on any configuration of a lender, the lender cannot submit or activate another one and cannot retire the held one; the platform retires or releases. Without this a new draft with a new callback token undoes the kill switch.
4. A `group_company` merchant owner kind (M2) was not added: it needs a change of two check constraints and of the contract; say if it is wanted.
5. The attestation is by one platform person (registry.verify). Make it two-person if the owner wants a stronger control; the table already records who and which evidence.
6. U53 closes the bypass inside the database. The internet-facing callback receiver still runs on the same `service_role` login as the worker (U53 note): give it its own login that can only execute the ingest functions (infrastructure and API change).

## 5. The real activation order (for `docs/operations/phase-08a-activation-gates.md`)

Proved with public functions only and two platform people (028, section 13):

1. Lender: onboarding gate activated and organisation active (existing).
2. Lender (`servicing.sync.manage`, aal2): `servicing.set_lms_connection(lender, 'file_import' or 'manual', 'active')`. Allowed before the switch is on.
3. Platform person A requests `servicing_sync` with evidence; platform person B (different) decides.
4. Same for `statements_noc` (needs servicing_sync).
5. Lender: reminder policy and the three English wordings (pre_due, due_day, overdue) drafted by `reminder.policy.manage`, approved by `reminder.template.approve` (a different person). Then `reminders` (needs servicing_sync).
6. Platform: key version 1 proposed and approved (two people); account format rule approved (two people); provider registered with authorisation evidence; Udofin and group accounts registered and verified; `payments.attest_own_account_list(evidence)`.
7. Lender account: registered by a registry manager, verified by a different verifier, settlement account requested by the lender (canonical masked reference) and linked by the registry manager; merchant claimed by the lender and verified by the platform.
8. Lender: payment configuration drafted (secret names in the lender namespace), submitted by a second person, approved by an approver. Then `payments` (needs an active production configuration, a verified registry entry, the attested own-account list).
9. Lender: `payments.set_mandate_policy` and rail limits in the configuration; then `mandates` (needs servicing_sync, payments checks, a mandate-capable provider, rails, the policy).

After a key rotation the own-account list must be attested again under the new key version before new lender accounts are verified or linked; existing links move automatically.

## 6. Risks seen and not fixed

- The callback receiver and the worker share the `service_role` login and direct DML on `tenancy.*` (U53 note): U53 is closed for the session-setting route, but a receiver compromise still holds the whole system role.
- The system role can still insert unverified settlement accounts directly (by design since migration 910); every other write needs the registry proof (now judged for every writer).
- `private.acct_text_unsafe` is a guard rail, not a scanner: letters spelled out, digits split by words, or numbers hidden in a language other than ASCII digits pass.
- Dates written with spaces or hyphens and nine or more digits (for example `2026-10-18-0001`) are refused in free text; this is the same rule as the contracts' `looksPersonal`.
- Only `suspend_payment_config`, `system_suspend_payment_config` and `revalidate_payment_configs` set a hold. The registry-revocation and merchant-revocation cascades suspend a configuration without a hold: the cause cannot be undone by the lender (a revoked settlement account and a revoked merchant claim are final) and validation keeps refusing the configuration.
- Mutation check: the function-level hold checks in `submit_payment_config` and `decide_payment_config` are redundant with the table trigger (same condition, same hint), so removing only one of them leaves 028 green. They are kept as the first, cheaper refusal; the trigger is the guard that every writer meets.
- Mutation check, second survivor: the explicit "registry entry is not verified" test in `private.guard_settlement_account` is redundant with the classification that follows it (an unverified entry is never `permitted`; same hint `settlement_account_unproven`). It is kept for the clearer message.
- Rail limits: a number or digit string of nine or more digits is accepted only under a key ending `_minor` (a longer number is indistinguishable from an account number). A configuration whose provider limits are written in whole rupees above 99,999,999 under another key name is refused; write them in paise under a `_minor` key.
- `servicing.set_lms_connection` and `private.feature_prerequisites_unmet` are redefined here from the bodies of migrations 911 and 915 (one extra branch and one extra reason). If the servicing builder redefines either in its own migration, the later migration number wins and must carry both changes (A42 branch; `own_account_list_not_attested` reason); check at merge.
- The helpers `private.registry_is_successor` and `private.own_account_list_attested` are security definer and executable by nobody but the owner (they are called only from security definer functions); the tests read the attestation state through a security definer helper of the test file.
