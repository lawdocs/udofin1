# Public footprint of the Udofin / Finbyx / LawDocsComply / Equator ecosystem, and how the four products should relate (as of 2026-10-10)

**Executive summary.** These four products have very little public presence, and one of the claims that matters most failed when checked. **Udofin.com** is indexed under a single page ("Udofin - Smart Loan Platform", described as AI-powered onboarding, document checks and tailored loan offers). The index shows no lender names, grievance officer, legal entity, Google Play listing or RE-published LSP listing. **Finbyx.com** has no indexed pages at all. **LawDocsComply.com** is the most developed public site. It describes a GRC SaaS for NBFCs and other financial institutions, covering RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, IFSCA, MCA, labour and data-protection compliance, plus risk, litigation and contract modules and "AI-assisted legal advisory" services. **equator.in does not appear to be the group's ADR platform.** Search engines index equator.in and www.equator.in as the site of _Equator Property Managers Pvt Ltd_, a Mumbai facility-management company. The group's public ODR platform appears to be **Equa / Equa.Law (equa.law)**. It screens claims with an "Equator AI" component, links to "LawDocs Online" and "Hire LawDocs Paralegal", and is run by the founder of LawDocs. No public evidence was found that Equa/Equator is recognised as a mediation service provider under the Mediation Act 2023, designated or graded as an arbitral institution, or empanelled on SEBI's ODR framework. The architecture must treat Equator as an **independent, separately consented data fiduciary**. It must never be a default or mandatory escalation step that could displace the borrower's RBI Integrated Ombudsman route. Its role is especially sensitive because Udofin plans to give FLDG and run collections, which gives it a direct economic interest in how borrower disputes turn out.

> **Method and limitations (read first).** In this run the WebFetch tool failed DNS resolution for every host (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`, even for rbi.org.in), and direct HTTPS was refused by the sandbox proxy (CONNECT 403). **No page was opened and no page source was read.** All site findings come from search-engine index results and snippets for these domains, retrieved on 2026-10-10. The shared web-search budget then ran out, so the regulatory statements below come from prior knowledge and are labelled **[likely]** or **[unverified]** unless a search result in this run supported them. The owner's logged-in product views were not available. Re-run the site checks from a normal browser before relying on any "absence" finding.

## Findings

### 1. Udofin (udofin.com)

- **What it claims.** The only indexed page is the homepage, titled **"Udofin - Smart Loan Platform"**. The index summary says it offers loans "powered by AI" covering onboarding, document checking and tailored loan offers [confirmed: search index] ([Udofin - Smart Loan Platform](https://www.udofin.com/) (accessed 2026-10-10)). Two separate searches, one restricted to the domain and one open, returned no other udofin.com URL. The searches looked for about, privacy, terms, grievance, partners and FAQ pages [confirmed: search index, negative result].
- **Modules/features visible publicly:** AI onboarding, document checks and personalised offers only [confirmed: snippet]. The index shows nothing about the planned product list (LAS, home, vehicle, LAP, payday, resolution finance and so on), eSign, eNACH or collections [unverified: may exist behind login].
- **Target customers:** retail borrowers, implied [interpretation].
- **Integrations mentioned:** none visible in the index.
- **Regulatory claims:** none visible. The index snippet does not say whether Udofin is a lender or an LSP, and does not name a partner RE [confirmed: snippet]. A tagline such as "Smart Loan Platform" with "tailored loan offers" and no RE named risks the LSP **presenting itself as the lender**. RBI's digital-lending framework prohibits this. The LSP/DLA must name the RE(s) and show the grievance officer [likely: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines 2022 / Digital Lending Directions 2025; not re-verified in this run].
- **Visible gaps/risks:** (a) no visible grievance redressal officer (GRO) name or contact; (b) no visible list of partner REs; (c) no legal entity name, CIN or registered address; (d) "AI" claims with no statement that the **credit decision rests with the RE**; (e) no privacy policy in the index [all: confirmed absent from search index only; unverified on the live site].
- **Registrations/listings:**
  - **Google Play:** a Play-restricted search for "Udofin" returned no listing [confirmed: search negative] ([Google Play search results](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.anyfin.app&hl=en_US) returned only similarly named apps) (accessed 2026-10-10).
  - **LSP partner of any NBFC/bank:** no RE web page naming Udofin as an LSP or DLA turned up [confirmed: search negative].
  - **RBI public DLA directory:** this could not be checked (RBI site unreachable, search budget exhausted). Since 2025, REs report their DLAs to RBI through CIMS and RBI publishes a public directory of DLAs reported by REs [likely: Digital Lending Directions 2025; not re-verified]. Udofin cannot appear there until a partner RE reports it, and no Play app was found. So Udofin is **almost certainly not listed** [interpretation].

### 2. Finbyx (finbyx.com)

The open search for "Finbyx loan management system NBFC" returned only competitors such as Nelito FinCraft, Bryt, Corpbiz and Finezza [confirmed: search negative] ([Decentro LMS list](https://decentro.tech/blog/loan-management-system/) (accessed 2026-10-10)).

- **Therefore unverified:** claimed modules (LOS/LMS, accounting, CIC reporting, RBI returns, collections), customers, certifications, legal entity and pricing.

CIC data submission is the **RE's** obligation as a credit institution and member of the credit information companies. Finbyx only prepares files on the RE's behalf.

**4.3 Legal status**

- **Mediation service provider (MSP) under the Mediation Act 2023:** no evidence was found that Equa or Equator is recognised by the Mediation Council of India or empanelled by a court or authority. A domain-restricted search returned only Equa's own blog and marketing [confirmed: search negative]. Under the Act, MSPs must be recognised by the Mediation Council of India, and the Act provides for online mediation, mediator disclosure of conflicts of interest, confidentiality and enforceable mediated settlement agreements [likely: Mediation Act 2023, Act No. 32 of 2023; section numbers not re-verified]. Some provisions came into force in stages from October 2023. September 2026 coverage of the India Digital ADR Summit describes India as still moving "from Mediation Act, 2023 to institutional practice" ([SCC Online, 2026-09-14](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/09/14/india-digital-adr-summit-2026-justice-sonia-gokani-mediation-act-2023/)), so the recognition regime may only be partly operational [unverified]. **Until it is recognised, Equator should not describe itself as an "MSP" or "recognised mediation institution"** [recommendation].
- **Arbitral institution:** no evidence of designation by the Supreme Court or a High Court for appointments under s.11, nor of grading. The 2019 amendments to the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996 provide for an Arbitration Council of India to grade institutions. Its operational status is unverified [unverified]. Equa's rules say it administers arbitrations seated in New Delhi. That is legally possible as private institutional arbitration, but it **confers no special regulatory standing** [interpretation].
- **ODR platform:** Equa operates as a private ODR platform [confirmed: self-description]. No evidence was found of empanelment on **SEBI's ODR framework**, which runs through MII-empanelled ODR institutions [unverified: SEBI list not checked]. **RBI does not "approve" ADR platforms for borrower grievances.** The statutory escalation route for complaints against REs is the **RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2021** [likely].
- **Risky claims for a group-affiliated ADR platform** [interpretation]:
  - (a) "AI decides disputes". An award must be a reasoned decision signed by the arbitrator(s) under the 1996 Act, and an AI cannot be an arbitrator. Equator AI should be limited to triage and drafting assistance, with a human neutral who decides.
  - (b) HIPAA and CCPA claims are irrelevant in India and may mislead.
  - (c) "Accredited/certified mediation training": accredited by whom? Under the 2023 Act, mediation institutes are recognised by the Council.
  - (d) Escrow handling of disputed sums may engage payment-system rules unless done through a bank escrow account.
  - (e) Family and custody matters bring in children's data and sensitive data under DPDP, so they need separate handling from fintech disputes.

**6.2 Integration styles** [recommendation]

- **Udofin ↔ RE (Finbyx or other LMS):**
  - Synchronous REST for eligibility and offer, application submit, KFS fetch and eSign status.
  - **Signed asynchronous events** (webhooks or a queue) for sanction, disbursement, repayment, bounce, DPD change and DLG invocation.
  - OAuth2 client-credentials or mTLS; idempotency keys; field-level allow-lists for each RE contract.
  - Finbyx must expose the **same API to any LSP**, with no Udofin-only back channels.
- **Udofin/Finbyx → LawDocsComply:** outbound evidence and metrics events plus pull APIs. No borrower PII crosses this boundary.
- **Udofin → Equator:**
  - A **borrower-initiated referral** through a deep link that carries a one-time, purpose-bound consent token.
  - Equator then pulls only the consented "referral package" (complaint summary, loan reference, documents the borrower chose).
  - Status flows back **only if the borrower consents**.
  - In the Udofin app, render Equator as a **separately branded, separate-origin web view** with its own privacy notice. Use no JavaScript bridge, so the Udofin app cannot read Equator content. This meets "no separate app" without merging data.
- **SSO:**
  - Group workforce SSO (one IdP for staff) is fine, **with segregation-of-duties rules**. No individual may hold Udofin collections/ops roles and Equator case-administration or neutral roles at the same time.
  - For borrowers, use **OIDC federation with pairwise (per-product) subject identifiers** and an explicit "link account" consent. No silent account linking and no shared borrower ID across products.
- **Infrastructure:**
  - Separate cloud projects or accounts per product (Supabase project or AWS account), separate KMS keys, and no shared database or cross-product joins.
  - Data stays in India.
  - Immutable cross-product audit logs record every data transfer and the consent ID that authorised it.

**6.3 Conflict-of-interest risks the architecture must mitigate** [interpretation unless marked]

1. **Udofin is economically a party in interest.** With 5% DLG and Udofin running collections, Udofin bears first loss and drives recoveries. Under RBI's June 2023 DLG guidelines the DLG cap is 5% of the portfolio [likely]. If a group-owned ADR forum hears disputes over those loans, its neutrality can be challenged.
   - For arbitration, the Arbitration and Conciliation Act s.12(5) and the Seventh Schedule make an arbitrator ineligible on certain relationships with a party or its affiliates. Section 12(1) and the Fifth Schedule require disclosure of circumstances raising justifiable doubts [likely].
   - The Supreme Court has invalidated unilateral appointment by an interested party: _Perkins Eastman v HSCC_ (2019) and the Constitution Bench in _CORE v ECI-SPIC-SMO-MCML (JV)_ (8 Nov 2024) [likely].
   - **Mitigations:**
     - No pre-dispute clause in retail loan agreements naming Equator.
     - ADR only by post-dispute opt-in.
     - Mandatory disclosure that Equator is a group affiliate.
     - A choice of an unaffiliated ODR provider.
     - An **independent appointing authority** for Udofin-linked matters.
     - A conflict-check service in Equator that holds a group-affiliation registry and blocks neutrals with links to Udofin, its REs or the group.
     - Published outcome statistics.
2. **Displacing the RBI Ombudsman.** Under RB-IOS 2021 a complaint is generally not maintainable if the same matter is pending or decided before an arbitrator, court or other forum [likely: not re-verified]. Routing "unresolved grievances" to Equator could therefore **extinguish the borrower's Ombudsman route**. Mitigations:
   - The grievance flow must offer the RE GRO, then RB-IOS (after 30 days or a rejection) **first and prominently**.
   - Equator is shown only as an optional alternative, with a warning about that consequence.
   - The RE, not Udofin, decides whether to offer ADR.
3. **Resolution, legal-expense and award-enforcement finance.** If the group finances settlements and enforcement of disputes decided on the group's own platform, that creates incentives to steer outcomes and to cross-sell. Mitigations:
   - Keep the products structurally separate: separate entities, no Equator data used for Udofin credit or marketing.
   - Bar Udofin from soliciting Equator parties.
   - Consider dropping award-enforcement finance where Equator issued the award.
4. **DPDP purpose limitation.** Each product processes for different purposes; a group "single customer view" or data lake breaches purpose limitation and the RBI rules on LSP data minimisation [likely]. Equator data (family, health, custody, children) is high-sensitivity. Mitigations:
   - Separate fiduciary registers and per-purpose consent artefacts (ready for Consent Managers).
   - Separate retention and erasure.
   - Data Principal rights handled separately by each fiduciary.
   - Note the phased commencement of the DPDP Rules 2025: most obligations about 18 months after the November 2025 notification [likely].
5. **Group GRC sees regulated entities' oversight of Udofin.** If partner REs use LawDocsComply, their LSP due-diligence, audit findings and complaint analytics about Udofin sit on a group platform. Mitigations:
   - Strict tenant isolation, with an encryption key per tenant that the customer can control.
   - No access by Udofin staff.
   - Contract terms disclosing the affiliation in the RE's outsourcing due diligence.
   - REs should not rely solely on a group tool for LSP oversight.
6. **Finbyx sees competitors' loan books.** An RE's Finbyx tenant may hold loans sourced by competing LSPs. Mitigations:
   - Information barriers: Finbyx staff separate from Udofin, product-level access logs.
   - Udofin's APIs return only loans that Udofin sourced and for which it holds borrower consent.

## Challenges to the owner's plan

1. **"Equator (equator.in)" seems to point to the wrong domain.**
   - _Problem:_ equator.in is indexed as a facility-management company's site. The group's ODR platform is publicly Equa/Equa.Law.
   - _Authority:_ sound practice; trademark law; DPDP security safeguards.
   - _Alternative:_ prove control of equator.in (WHOIS, DNS, TLS) or use the actual domain. Do trademark clearance for "Equator".
2. **Escalating unresolved grievances to a group-owned ADR platform.**
   - _Problem:_ this can bypass or extinguish the borrower's RBI Ombudsman route, and the forum is affiliated with Udofin, which bears first loss and collects.
   - _Authority:_ RB-IOS 2021 [likely]; A&C Act s.12 and Schedules; _CORE_ (2024).
   - _Alternative:_ RE GRO → RB-IOS is the default path. Equator is opt-in after the dispute arises, with an affiliation disclosure and an unaffiliated alternative. Use no pre-dispute arbitration clause in retail loan documents.
3. **"No separate app" for Equator.**
   - _Problem:_ this is fine for user experience, but it must not merge data or identity.
   - _Authority:_ DPDP purpose limitation; Mediation Act confidentiality [likely].
   - _Alternative:_ a separate-origin, separately branded web view with its own notice and consent, and pairwise borrower IDs.
4. **Resolution finance and legal-expense/award-enforcement finance linked to Equator outcomes.**
   - _Problem:_ incentives to steer outcomes and cross-sell.
   - _Alternative:_ structural separation, no use of Equator data for credit or marketing, and drop finance tied to Equator-issued awards. Obtain a legal opinion.
5. **AI deciding disputes (Equa's public copy).**
   - _Problem:_ an award must be the human tribunal's reasoned, signed decision.
   - _Alternative:_ restrict Equator AI to triage and drafting, and correct the public copy.
6. **Public claims across the group.**
   - _Problem:_ Equa claims non-profit status, HIPAA/CCPA/SOC 2/ISO compliance and "accredited" training. LawDocs offers "legal advisory" as a company. Udofin's homepage names no lender.
   - _Authority:_ Consumer Protection Act 2019 and CCPA guidance on misleading advertising and dark patterns (2023); Advocates Act 1961; RBI digital-lending rules on lender disclosure [likely].
   - _Alternative:_ keep a claims register with evidence (LawDocsComply can host it), remove unsupported claims, and name REs upfront.
7. **Group tools in the RE's oversight chain.**
   - _Problem:_ if partner REs use LawDocsComply and Finbyx, the group sees their oversight of Udofin and competitors' data.
   - _Authority:_ RBI MD on Outsourcing of IT Services (2023) [likely].
   - _Alternative:_ disclose the affiliation in RE due diligence, isolate tenants, apply information barriers, and give Udofin no privileged access.
8. **Launch readiness of the public footprint.**
   - _Problem:_ there is no indexed RE list, GRO or privacy page for Udofin; no Play listing; finbyx.com is unindexed.
   - _Alternative:_ prerender the disclosure pages and publish a sitemap. Launch only after the RE lists Udofin and reports the DLA to RBI.
